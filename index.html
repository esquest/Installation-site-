Why Esquest

The eye comes from the studio

Esquest Installations grew out of Esquest Studio, a photography, video, and design practice in Long Beach. Jose has spent years behind a camera and behind a laptop, framing shots and laying out pages, and he has hung gallery shows where every inch of wall mattered. That background shapes how he installs. Spacing, center height, sightlines, and how a room reads as a whole are the real decisions. The mounting hardware is the easy part. He brings the same eye to a living room wall that he would bring to a gallery opening.

Questions

A few things people ask

Do you patch holes?

Yes. Old holes get patched and touched up as part of the job, so the wall is left clean.

Do you supply the mount and hardware?

In most cases, yes. If you already bought a mount or hardware, bring it along and Jose will check that it's right for the wall and the piece before installing.

Can you hang on plaster, brick, or concrete?

Yes. Each surface needs its own hardware and approach, and that gets figured out ahead of time, before the day of install.

Do you work with renters?

Yes. Jose uses methods that keep the wall in good shape for a security deposit. Text a photo and mention it's a rental, and he'll talk through the options.

How far ahead do I need to book?

A few days is usually enough. Evenings and weekends fill up faster, so it helps to reach out early if the timing matters.

Do you take office jobs?

Yes. Branding walls, whiteboards, signage, and TV installs for offices are regular work, not an exception.
