# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Single self-contained HTML page published as a claude.ai Artifact (CSP: scripts only from allowlisted CDNs or inlined; Google Fonts only). React for embedded Stageshow demos is bundled and inlined.

## Users
One person: the owner-operator of Esquest Installations (TV mounting and art/mirror installation, Long Beach, LA and Orange County). They do the installs, quotes, website and marketing themselves. Pretty new to Claude Code: has installed skills and asked questions, has not built much yet. Opens the deck equally on a phone between jobs and on a laptop for sit-down learning sessions.

## Product Purpose
The Skill Deck is a playful personal training page for learning Claude and Claude Code: short "reps", each a copy-paste prompt, in two tracks for now (Think with Claude, Level up Claude Code), plus a Studio Lab for ideating a creative studio and collecting design and art references. More tracks will be added later. Success: the owner does one rep at a time, enjoys coming back, and each rep changes how they work.

## Positioning
Built from this owner's real setup (their connected tools and installed skills) and their interest in starting a creative studio. It should feel like a fun site, not a course or a deck.

## Operating Context
The installation business, job loop and website tracks were removed at the owner's request. Connected: Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Notion, GitHub, Adobe for creativity, Unsplash, Claude Docs, Postiz. Blocked in cloud sessions: Figma, Canva, Slack, HubSpot, Asana, Linear, ClickUp, monday. Repo: a single index.html marketing site.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Progress is per-browser (localStorage). Copy-to-clipboard prompts. No real numbers about the business exist yet; never invent revenue, jobs or reviews. Beginner level: explain terms plainly, start with the easiest reps.

## Brand Commitments
Site voice: plain, confident, no hype ("We don't just hang it. We make it look right."). Name: Esquest Installations.

## Evidence on Hand
Site copy in index.html. No testimonials, prices or job data on hand; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
- One rep at a time: the next action is always obvious.
- Beginner-first: plain words, no unexplained jargon.
- Fun first: motion and play (three.js wall, idea spinner) keep them coming back.
- Works one-handed on a phone and as a sit-down workspace on a laptop.
